# S23-PL2 bugs, deviations and decisions for Kanna

One entry per issue: what happened, what you tried (one bounded attempt), status.
Also list here: every deviation from the prototype or contract (with the reason), Presales export (decision for Kanna), BU scoping (deferred, Kanna's call).

## Decisions for Kanna

- **K1 · Presales export (B12, D20).** Presales still cannot export: the new `GET /api/pipeline/export` uses the same role set as the old Reports export (Presales gets 403, tested in `api/tests/test_s23_pl2_export.py`), and the page hides "↓ Export view" for Presales-only users. Status: unchanged, waiting for your decision.
- **K2 · BU scoping (B12).** No business-unit scoping was added: every user who can open the Pipeline sees all three business units. Status: deferred, your call.
- **K3 · "Pending approvals" vs the SOW board (B7).** The card counts DEALS (HubSpot deals whose most recently updated SOW is in review · delivery/hr, in review · finance/legal or CEO exception). The SOW board counts approval PACKAGES, includes the local SOW-upload deals and is scoped to the reader. At discovery the board showed 1 (a package on a local SOW-upload deal) and the Pipeline 0. Status: reported; your call whether the two should match.
- **K4 · Command Center tiles (B2).** "Open opportunity value" is now the Pipeline's own open number (count and USD value, same filter function, link to the Pipeline). The stricter number (USD only, weighted, no SOW or engagement link) keeps its value under the label "Not yet in the SOW flow" and now also leaves out deals with a live SOW, as the directive describes it. Staging's value does not change (no open deal has a SOW or an engagement). Status: built; please confirm the wording.
- **K5 · Where "Run sync now" lives (F3).** The prototype points administrators to Settings → Integrations, which has no run control. The button sits in the Sync health panel instead, shown only to SystemAdmin, and the panel's recovery sentence says "from this panel". Status: built; your call.

## Deviations from the prototype or the contract

### API (contract `docs/reference/openapi-pipeline.yaml`)

- **V1 · Pack zip.** `~/Downloads/dealgate-s23-pipeline-pack.zip` (10:56) holds the earlier PL2 directive (17,169 bytes, 10:53). The updated directive (18,015 bytes, 13:19) is in `~/Downloads/dealgate-s23-pipeline-pack (1).zip`. Both of your unzip commands were run against the `(1)` zip: first `unzip -n … -x README.md`, which adds only missing files, then `unzip -o … docs/directives/s23-pl2-pipeline-redesign.md`. No other repo file was overwritten, and `docs/bugs/s23-pl1.md` is untouched.
- **V2 · Money is a decimal string** (e.g. `"21596444.14"`), not a JSON number. CLAUDE.md rule 2 (Decimal, no floats) outranks the contract's `number`; every existing Pipeline amount is already a decimal string.
- **V3 · `stage_id` is the stage's position, not the HubSpot stage id.** Positions run 1–9 in the pipeline's display order, and Hold is 9 on staging. HubSpot's stage ids are 10-digit numbers (e.g. 1038193692), which the contract's 1–9 range cannot hold. Hold is recognised by its label "Hold" on a stage that is not closed. If the mirror ever gives several pipelines, positions are per pipeline. A deal whose stage is not in the live mirror gets `stage_id: null`, appears in no stage count or board lane, and shows its HubSpot stage name. No such deal exists on staging.
- **V4 · Additive fields.** None of these is in the contract; all are additive:
  - `options`: deal owners, account owners, business units with regions, and SOW states.
  - `tab_counts`, `today`, `metrics.open_value_usd.deals` and `metrics.agreement_gap_clients.any_agreement_files`.
  - On DealRow: `status` (open/hold/won/lost), `stage_label`, `sow_state_label`, `days_since_activity`, `next_action.id`, `pipeline_label`, and `hubspot_deal_id` (used only for the HubSpot link, never shown).
  - On ClientRow: `days_since_activity` and `hubspot_company_id` (link only).
  - Stage counts carry `full_label`.
  - The workspace accepts a `client` query parameter, used by the client preview to list one client's deals. `account_owner=former` is also accepted.
- **V5 · Metric drills keep the selected stages.** The prototype's card click clears them, which breaks "the number on the card equals the list" when a stage is selected (prototype: card 19, list 109). The deal-metric drills also switch to the Opportunities tab, so the list they open is a list of deals. The agreement-gap drill opens Clients and forces "include clients without matching deals" off.
- **V6 · Focus counts ignore the current filters.** Each count is the number of open deals under that view's own preset, matching the prototype. The contract does not say which population the focus counts use.
- **V7 · "Activity · oldest first" puts deals with no activity date last**, as the contract says. The prototype puts them first.
- **V8 · Non-USD amounts** keep their currency on the row and sort by raw amount. They are left out of every USD total and of each stage's known value, and do not count as "no amount". There is no conversion.
- **V9 · SOW state** comes from the most recently updated live SOW. "Updated" is taken as the latest of: SOW creation, version uploads, and package submit/release/void (`sow` has no `updated_at`). The labels are the nine states the code really derives, so the prototype's single "In review" appears as "In review · delivery/hr" and "In review · finance/legal". "+N more" appears when a deal has several SOWs. Today the app never creates a second SOW for a deal: every creation path loads the deal's SOW with `scalar_one_or_none`, and staging has 0 HubSpot deals with a SOW.
- **V10 · Owners.**
  - An owner id that is missing from the mirror counts as a former HubSpot user. Archived owners' names are never returned to the Pipeline, which shows them as "Former HubSpot user".
  - Owner filter values are HubSpot owner ids; before, they were DealGate user ids.
  - The CSV prints "Unassigned" for no owner and "Former HubSpot user" for archived owners.
- **V11 · CSV.** The new export and the four old ones prefix `'` to a cell that starts with = + - @ tab or CR, except a cell that is a plain number (`-250.50` stays a number in Excel and can never run as a formula). The old exports get no BOM: their tests parse them with `csv.DictReader`.
- **V12 · Extra 422s.** These return 422 with a message: `stage` outside 1–9, a `sow` value that is neither a state nor `pending`, and a custom range whose start is after its end. A wrong `page_size` returns exactly "page_size must be one of 25, 50, 100".
- **V13 · Unnamed or client-less deals.** A deal with no HubSpot name is titled "Unnamed deal", never its id. A deal with no client has `client {id: null, name: null}` and no client row.
- **V14 · `hubspot_region` may be null.** A deal with no HubSpot region counts as unmapped, and Settings lists it separately as "deals without a region".
- **V15 · Business units in Settings** (`GET/PUT /api/settings/business-units`, SystemAdmin only):
  - The response adds `deals_without_region` and the BU list.
  - Saving an identical map writes nothing and records no audit.
  - Rows are ordered by HubSpot value; unmapped values by deal count.
- **V16 · Owner link support** (additive):
  - `GET /api/admin/users` rows carry the linked owner's name, state and link source. Admins also see archived owners' names here.
  - `GET /api/admin/hubspot-owners` feeds the override picker.
  - Choosing "No HubSpot owner" unlinks; it is stored as an admin decision and never re-linked automatically.
  - Auto-linking is a case-insensitive exact email match with an active owner. It is skipped when the email matches two users or the owner is already linked.
  - No data migration links staging users. The first sign-in or the next nightly owner sync does it, and until then "My opportunities" uses the same email match read-only.
- **V17 · Personal workspace.**
  - Views and groups list in creation order.
  - View-name uniqueness covers all of a user's live custom views, old and new pages, case-insensitive.
  - `DELETE /api/pipeline/groups/{id}/deals` also takes `deal_ids` in the query string, because proxies may drop a DELETE body.
  - "Watching" means deal watches; the old client-page star (client watches) does not count.
  - Watch add and remove now write audit rows (rule 5), as do user-preference changes.
- **V18 · The Command Center.** The grid is now 3 columns × 2 rows (six tiles).
  - **Open opportunity value** counts exactly the list its link opens. A raw HubSpot region in the Command Center filter becomes its business unit, so both IT Avalon values count all of IT Avalon. The tile names the unit, for example "39 open deals in IT Avalon".
  - **Owner scope.** An owner is translated to their HubSpot owner. When that is impossible (no HubSpot owner link, or the Unassigned placeholder), the figure is shown without a link and without the claim "Same number as the Pipeline".
  - **Not yet in the SOW flow** (the tile, the lifecycle row and the brief) opens `/pipeline?status=open&sow=none` plus the scope. The tile says what still differs: it is weighted and leaves out deals linked to an engagement.
  - **Contract change.** These links can now be null: `metrics.open_opportunity_href`, `tiles.pipeline.href`, the brief and lifecycle hrefs. The response adds `metrics.open_opportunity_business_units`.

## Bugs found while building and testing

- **G1 · Second saved view fails (B11 root cause).** The unique key `(owner_id, key, is_builtin)` caught every custom view, because all custom views have `key='custom'`. The second save hit the global IntegrityError handler and returned 409 "That change conflicts with a record that already exists.", not a 500 as the discovery said. The fix:
  - Migration 0070 replaces the key with a built-in-only index plus a case-insensitive name index.
  - The new page writes `key='pipeline'`.
  - A duplicate name returns "You already have a view with this name."
  
  Status: fixed and tested (`test_s23_pl2_personal.py`, Postgres and SQLite).
- **G2 · HubSpot-created users could not save views, groups or watches (B11).** Personal rows were keyed on the login id, which differs from the DealGate user id for invited and HubSpot-created users. With foreign keys enforced (as on Postgres):
  - `GET /saved-views` and `POST /saved-views` returned 409.
  - A repeated `POST /watchlist` returned 500.
  - Next actions were refused.
  
  First reproduced with an FK-enforcing test engine, then fixed: every personal route now resolves the DealGate user id, and the watch insert is idempotent. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G3 · Sales-only users got 403 on client pages (B12)** reached from the Pipeline. Reproduced first, then fixed in `_access_or_forbid`. Status: fixed and tested (`test_s23_pl2_access.py`).
- **G4 · The Pipeline workspace is slow on Postgres with correlated next-action subqueries.** About 100 ms extra at 672 deals. They were replaced by one ranked join: the population statement went from ~130 ms to ~35 ms on local Postgres. Status: fixed.
- **G5 · The kit's class merger dropped the theme's font sizes.** `cn("text-section text-text")` returned `text-text`, so kit titles, labels, buttons and inputs lost their size app-wide. `web/src/lib/cn.ts` now registers the theme sizes with tailwind-merge. Status: fixed (pre-existing, affects every page).
- **G6 · Dialog and drawer overlays did not dim the page.** `bg-executive/50` produces no CSS on a `var()` colour; the overlays now use `bg-executive` with `opacity-*`. The indeterminate "Select current page" checkbox looked checked; it now shows a dash. Status: fixed (kit, pre-existing).
- **G7 · Rows briefly drawn with the wrong layout.** After a tab or layout switch, the previous answer could be drawn as the new tab's rows. The records panel now waits until the answer fits. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G8 · Next-action editor race.** The editor re-read the action after its own save. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G9 · Two error panels on a failed load** (page and records). Status: fixed, one panel in the records area.
- **G10 · Sync polling could be misled by a fast browser clock** (compared with the server's attempt time). Status: fixed: polling compares server times only.
- **G11 · `resolve_viewer` treated an owner already linked to another user as an email match.** Status: fixed and tested (`test_s23_pl2_viewer.py`).
- **G12 · Toolbar status select stretched full width.** Status: fixed.
- **G13 · Test-harness issues** (MissingGreenlet on expired ORM objects; SQLite dropping non-UTC offsets; Postgres planner without statistics in the cost test). Status: fixed in the tests.
- **G15 · A deal without a client rendered an empty client link ("↗").** Found in the local full-stack check (real API on Postgres behind the real page). It now shows "No client" with no link, and the Upload SOW link omits the client. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G14 · Leak gate blind spots (T11, D24).** The gate saw only the bot's fixtures, and never saved views, groups or watches. It now checks the bot's own views, groups and watches on the old and new routes; it ran clean against staging. The one leftover test client is **"smoke smoke 20261003T174232Z"** (created 2026-10-03, live, no live fixture grant). The gate cannot see it because the bot can't. Status: reported, not deleted, as asked.

### Pre-merge review (four reviewers, 2026-10-14 evening); every finding was fixed before the merge to main

- **G16 · Access-token users lost their personal data (blocker; R1, R2).** The web app's Cognito access token carries no email. Reads could not find a user row that intake or an invite created and writes adopted (id ≠ sub). For those users, saved views, groups, watches, preferences, "My opportunities" and next-action edit rights vanished on reload. Staging missed it: the bot's row was created by its own sign-in, and tests sign in with a real email.
  - Fix: migration `20261014_0071_cognito_sub` stores the login on the adopted row (`ensure_user`). Reads match on it, with the same Cognito lookup as `ensure_user` (cached, off the event loop) before the first write.
  - Test: `api/tests/test_s23_pl2_access_token_identity.py` builds the caller from access-token claims. It fails on the old code and passes now; the reviewers' probes also pass.
  - Status: fixed and tested.
- **G17 · Overdue card denominator (blocker; R3).** "of N deals with a next action" was counted on a list that still had the attention filter the overdue drill replaces, so the overdue deals were not among the N. It is now counted on the card's own list. `test_s23_pl2_metric_drill.py` asserts the subset over every combination. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G18 · Command Center open number vs its link (blocker; R2, R3, R4).** With a raw region chosen in the Command Center, the tile counted that region while its link opened the whole business unit (V18). Tests: `test_s23_pl2_command_center.py`, plus the reviewers' probe, which now shows tile = list for every region. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G19 · Stricter tile link (R4).** "Not yet in the SOW flow" opened every open deal. It now opens the closest Pipeline list and says what differs. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G20 · Revenue outlook regression (R2).** Leaving SOW-linked deals out of the stricter set also dropped them from "unscheduled weighted pipeline excluded from bars". That note is computed as on main again. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G21 · Owner-sync link could fail the nightly deal sync (R2).** The owner link could deadlock with a sign-in, and the deadlock or any other error in the link step could fail the whole sync. The bulk link now runs after the owner mirror is committed, in its own transaction; it logs and leaves users unlinked for the next run. A sign-in caught in the same race carries on unlinked. This includes a real two-connection deadlock test on Postgres. Status: fixed and tested. Residual: the lock order can still deadlock; it is now harmless.
- **G22 · Expired-row 500 on a link race (R2).** A unique-index race in the email link returned an expired user row, which then failed with MissingGreenlet. The row is now refreshed. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G23 · 0070 downgrade (R2).** The downgrade failed once a user had two Pipeline views or an archived one. Custom views are now re-keyed per row before the old key is restored; the docstring has the restore statement. Tested by a Postgres round trip. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G24 · Back-button trap (R4).** Correcting a page past the end pushed a history entry, so Back could not leave the Pipeline. It now replaces the entry. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G25 · Raw ids in the sync panel (R4).** The panel printed server reasons that can hold HubSpot stage ids (V35). Status: fixed and tested.
- **G26 · Client preview facts (R4).** The preview of a client with no HubSpot deals lost its account owner and agreements. They now come from the row. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G27 · No-currency deals shown as USD (R4).** Fixed as in V35. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G28 · Stale sync baseline (R4).** "Run sync now" could compare against a stale attempt time. It reads sync health just before triggering. Status: fixed and tested.
- **G29 · Lost drilled status (R4 nit).** After a metric drill with stages selected, removing the last stage restored the old status instead of the drilled one. Status: fixed and tested.
