# S4 E7 — CEO exception brief + approve/reject/return

## User story
When any US or India floor fails on a submitted package, the system drafts
a CEO brief (client, scope, team, revenue, cost, US/India/blended GM
against floors, the price increase needed to reach policy, the gross-profit
shortfall in dollars, alternatives considered, Finance's and Delivery's
recommendations, plus the account owner's business rationale — the AI can
tidy the rationale but not invent it). The CEO approves (with conditions +
expiry), rejects, or returns for changes. Approvals are never reused
across packages.

## Acceptance tests
- Given a package's US or India floor fails, when it moves to
  `pending_ceo_exception`, then a CEO brief is drafted with:
  - client name and context
  - scope + team summary
  - revenue + cost per US/India + blended
  - GM per component with floor pass/fail
  - price uplift required to reach policy (per component)
  - gross-profit shortfall in USD at the proposed price
  - alternatives considered (from the account owner's input; blank if none)
  - Finance rec + Delivery rec (from their approvals)
- Given the account owner has not written the business rationale, then
  the CEO cannot approve — 422 with the missing field.
- Given the CEO approves with conditions + valid_until, then the exception
  is recorded with those constraints; package moves to `ready_to_sign`.
- Given the CEO rejects or returns, then the package goes back to
  `SOWDraft` and the account owner is notified.
- Given the package is later voided (via change-voids-approval), then the
  CEO exception's `valid_until` becomes moot; the next package requires a
  new CEO decision.

## Data touched
- New: `ceo_exception` (id, package_id, decision, rationale_text,
  conditions_text, valid_until, decided_by, decided_at, brief_json).

## Roles
- Read brief: all governance roles.
- Write rationale: account owner only.
- Approve/reject/return: CEO or CEO delegate (recorded, time-bound).

## Out of scope
- CEO delegate management UI (separate story if the CEO wants it in-app;
  otherwise the system admin sets it).
